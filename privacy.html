Privacy Policy – ClipHunter Studio

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Operator / contact: cank9204 (private individual developer, non-commercial), cank9204@gmail.com

ClipHunter Studio ("the App") is a desktop application that runs on the operator's own computer. It helps the operator edit video clips into vertical short videos and, after explicit confirmation of each post, publish them to the operator's own TikTok account through TikTok's official Content Posting API.

1. Data we process

  • TikTok account data: When you log in with TikTok, the App receives an access token, a refresh token and your TikTok open ID, plus your display name and the posting options available to your account (creator info). Scopes requested: user.info.basic, video.publish, video.upload.
  • Video files and metadata you create in the App (titles, descriptions, hashtags).
  • Rights records: notes about permissions granted by content creators (e.g. platforms allowed, date of consent).

2. Where data is stored

All data is stored locally on the operator's computer (a local SQLite database and local files). The App has no server of its own; we do not operate a backend that collects your data. Tokens are stored in the local database and can be removed at any time with "Log out" in the App settings.

3. How data is used

Data is used only to (a) show your TikTok account name in the posting dialog, (b) upload the video you explicitly confirm, and (c) check publishing status. We do not sell, share or use TikTok data for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

4. Sharing

Video files and post settings are sent only to TikTok (via open.tiktokapis.com) when you confirm a post. No other third party receives TikTok user data from the App.

5. Retention and deletion

Data stays on the local machine until you delete it. Log out in the App to delete TikTok tokens; delete the data folder to remove everything. You can also revoke the App's access in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Apps and services.

6. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (e.g. GDPR) you may request access, correction or deletion of your data. Because data is stored locally, you control it directly; for any request contact cank9204@gmail.com.

7. Children

The App is not directed at persons under 18.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
